Common questions

Robot mower surveys in Lancashire

A few things people in the Ribble Valley and wider North West usually ask before they get in touch.

Do you cover my part of Lancashire?

We work from the Ribble Valley and Clitheroe across Lancashire, including Preston, Blackburn, Burnley, Lancaster, Chorley and Accrington, plus neighbouring parts of the North West. Email your postcode if you are unsure.

What do I send for a free robot mower survey?

Your postcode, a few photos of the lawn, an idea of size if you have it, and a note about slopes or split lawns. We will tell you the next step.

Which robot mower brands do you fit?

We work with Mammotion, Kress, Segway Navimow and Husqvarna Automower. The machine comes after the survey, not before.

Is the first look actually free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation for that initial look at your lawn.
