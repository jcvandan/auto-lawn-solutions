Free lawn survey

Not sure whether a robot mower will work on your lawn?

Ask us to take an initial look. No obligation — just straightforward advice from an independent specialist.

What to send

A few details are enough to start

Where appropriate, an initial remote assessment can be carried out using satellite imagery. For larger or more complex properties we will recommend a site visit.

  • Postcode / property location
  • A few photographs of the lawn
  • Approximate lawn size if known
  • Steep areas, multiple lawns or awkward sections

Photographs help us judge slopes, trees, passages and charging options.
