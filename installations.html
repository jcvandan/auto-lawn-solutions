Example case study

Large sloping property

Challenge A large area with banks and uneven ground. It used to be a long weekly job on a ride-on.
Mower selected AWD robotic mower after a site visit.
Why it was chosen The extra grip for banks, and enough capacity to cover the whole area.
Setup Mapped on site, charger placed, and a handover on the app.
Result The lawn is cut through the week. No long session on the ride-on.

Example case study

Large open lawn

Challenge A large open lawn that needed cutting all through the growing season.
Mower selected RTK robotic mower, mapped on site.
Why it was chosen Accurate virtual boundaries on an open lawn, without burying perimeter wire.
Setup RTK reference set, zones mapped, schedule fitted around how the garden is used.
Result The lawn stays even. The owner does not have to think about mowing much.
