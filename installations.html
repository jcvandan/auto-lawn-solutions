Recent installations / case studies

Real properties. Real robot mowers.

Each case study follows a simple format: property challenge, mower selected, why it was chosen, setup and result. The two examples below are illustrative placeholders until live installation photography is available.

Example case study

Large sloping property

Challenge Large mowing area with gradients and uneven terrain — previously a long weekly job on a ride-on.
Mower selected AWD robotic mower following a site assessment.
Why it was chosen All-wheel drive for banks and mixed ground, with capacity for the full mowing area.
Setup On-site mapping, charging station positioning and a full customer handover.
Result Automated lawn maintenance without spending hours each week on a ride-on mower.

Example case study

Large open lawn

Challenge Large open grass area requiring regular mowing throughout the growing season.
Mower selected RTK robotic mower professionally configured and mapped.
Why it was chosen Precision navigation on an open lawn with virtual boundaries rather than perimeter wire.
Setup RTK reference, zone mapping and schedule set around how the household uses the garden.
Result Consistent automated mowing with minimal homeowner involvement.
